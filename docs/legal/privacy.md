# Privacy Notice: AI Receptionist

Last updated: 2026-11-10 (version 2026-11-10-v1)

## 1. Who we are and how to reach us
The AI Receptionist is operated by Wright AI Solutions, Tempe, Arizona, USA ("we"). It answers phone calls for a business: today Wright AI Solutions' own line, and after hours for Mountain High Disposal ("the business"). For calls to a customer's line, the business decides why calls are handled and is the controller; we act as its processor. For our own line we are the controller.
Contact for privacy questions and deletion requests: privacy@wright-ai-solutions.com

## 2. What we collect
- Your phone number (caller ID) and call metadata (time, length, how the call ended).
- A recording of the call and a transcript, because you speak to an AI assistant.
- A call summary written by AI.
- Booking details you give: name, email, reason for the call, chosen time.
- Messages you text us, the text we send you, and whether you opted out.
- Your stated consent record: that we told you it is an AI and that the call is recorded (time and wording version), and why we texted you.
- Anything else you choose to say on the call. Please don't share sensitive details (health, financial account numbers); we don't ask for them.

## 3. Why we use it
To answer your call, answer questions from an approved list, book appointments, transfer you to a person, take a message, text you back if we missed your call (one text per missed call, once a day at most, 8am to 9pm local time, never after you reply STOP), keep the service safe and working, and meet legal duties. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell or share it for advertising.

## 4. Who receives it
Our service providers, only to run the service: Twilio (phone and SMS), Vapi (voice agent platform), ElevenLabs (synthetic voice), the AI language model provider used through Vapi (currently Anthropic), Cal.com (appointments), our hosting provider (Fly.io), our database host (PostgreSQL), and Cloudflare R2 (recording storage, planned). The business whose line you called also receives call summaries, messages and recordings. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

## 5. Where it goes
These providers are mainly in the United States and may process data in other countries. The service is intended for callers in the United States.

## 6. How long we keep it
Recordings: 90 days. Transcripts, summaries and booking details: 12 months. Text logs: 12 months. Opt-out records: kept until you ask us to remove them, so we keep honouring them. A booking's calendar entry lives with Cal.com under its terms. Shorter or longer legal holds apply only if the law requires it. (The automated deletion job is being built; until it runs, deletion is done by hand on request.)

## 7. Your choices and rights
You may ask to see, correct or delete the data held about your phone number, or to stop texts. Text STOP at any time, or email us at the address above; we reply within 30 days. We verify that you control the number first.

## 8. Security and AI
Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is limited to the operator. We turn off provider model training on your calls wherever the provider allows it (recorded in docs/legal/vendors.md). The AI can make mistakes; a person reviews messages and bookings it flags as unclear.

## 9. Children
The service is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect data about them on purpose. If a child calls, ask us to delete it.

## 10. Recording and voice
Every call starts with the statement that this is an AI assistant and the call is recorded. If you don't want to be recorded, hang up and use the business's other contact method. We do not create voiceprints.

## 11. Changes
We update this notice with a new date and version, and keep prior versions. Material changes are communicated to customers by email.
