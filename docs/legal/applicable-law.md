# Applicable law (L12, L23). Reviewed 2026-11-10
Not legal advice; lawyer review is pending (see STATE.md).
- TCPA, 47 U.S.C. §227, and the FCC Feb 2024 ruling: AI voices are "artificial voice". Inbound-only calls; no outbound calls are built. Any outbound AI call needs prior express written consent first.
- CTIA and A2P 10DLC carrier rules: STOP/HELP handling, brand and campaign registration, privacy URL in the registration.
- Call recording: federal one-party consent (18 U.S.C. §2511); Arizona one-party (A.R.S. §13-3005); all-party states (CA Penal Code §632, FL §934.03, WA RCW 9.73.030, PA, IL, MD, MA and others). Rule used: every caller is treated as in an all-party state, so the first sentence of every call states AI and recording.
- California BOT Act (B&P §17941): the agent discloses it is an AI at the start.
- State privacy acts: thresholds not met for the pilot (small volume, no data sales); revisit at scale. Illinois BIPA: no voiceprints are made.
- Arizona breach law A.R.S. §18-552; other states' notice laws apply to affected callers.
- FTC Act §5 (honest claims) and the FTC testimonial rule, 16 CFR 465 (client result removed from the FAQ).
- Coverage decision (L23): US callers only. Text-back goes to US (+1) numbers only. Non-US callers get the same AI disclosure; no text-back. No GDPR/CASL targeting.
