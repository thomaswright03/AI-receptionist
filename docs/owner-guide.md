# Your AI receptionist: owner guide

## What it does
When someone calls your business number, an AI assistant answers. It says it is an AI and that the call is recorded. It answers
questions from the answers you approved, books a call on your calendar, passes the call to you when it must, and takes a message when
it can't. If a call is missed, the caller gets one short text (only between about 9am and 7pm, never to someone who said STOP).

## What you will get
- **An email after every call** saying what happened, with a summary and the recording link.
- **A text only when something needs you now**: an urgent message, a booking that needs checking, or a missed call. If your phone can't
  be texted, the same alert comes by email instead.
- **A weekly email on Monday morning** with the week's totals and anything broken.

## Controls (text these from your own cell to the business number)
- **PAUSE**: calls ring your cell instead of the receptionist. Use it any time you would rather take calls yourself.
- **RESUME**: calls go back to the receptionist.
- **STATUS**: what happened in the last 24 hours and anything that is broken.
- **HELP**: this list.

Do not text STOP to the business number: that is for callers.

## If something seems wrong
1. Text **PAUSE**. Calls ring your cell at once.
2. Text **STATUS** to see what the receptionist thinks is wrong.
3. If it doesn't answer at all, the phone line is set to ring your cell by itself when the service is down.
4. Tell the person who set this up (Wright AI Solutions).

## Messages that were saved but not sent
If STATUS says a message was "saved but could not be sent", ask Wright AI Solutions for it: it is stored, not lost.
